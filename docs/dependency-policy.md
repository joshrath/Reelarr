# Dependency policy and finding review (#31)

The SHA-pinned EmbarkStudios/cargo-deny-action v2.1.1 bundles cargo-deny 0.20.2.
Local and CI policy command: `cargo deny --locked check --warn unmaintained`.
The graph includes every workspace crate, all features, dev/build dependencies and
all target platforms (no platform exclusions). The lockfile is not regenerated by
this check. Vulnerabilities and unsound advisories are denied; the narrow
unmaintained lint override only warns, as requested. RustSec is fetched normally,
not disabled or frozen. Sources permit crates.io only; git/unknown registries fail.

## Reviewed license baseline

- MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, Zlib, Unicode-3.0 and
  Unicode-DFS-2016: permissive/GPLv3-compatible; retain applicable copyright,
  permission and attribution texts. Apache-2.0 also requires relevant NOTICE and
  modification notices and its patent conditions.
- MPL-2.0: file-level source/notices and the secondary-license mechanism permit
  GPLv3 combinations; review any Exhibit B incompatible-with-secondary-licenses
  marking rather than assuming metadata alone resolves it.
- GPL-3.0-only / GPL-3.0-or-later and LGPL-3.0-only / LGPL-3.0-or-later are the
  **only** GNU variants allowed. Preserve corresponding source and license notices;
  LGPL requires relinking/reverse-engineering rights as applicable. A -only
  dependency constrains the combined distribution to GPLv3. This is not approval
  for arbitrary LGPL/GPL/AGPL versions, deprecated aliases or unknown licenses.
